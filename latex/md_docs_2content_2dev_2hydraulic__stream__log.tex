Overview}{1. Overview}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md49}
While particle-\/based hydraulic erosion ({\ttfamily hydraulic\+\_\+particle}) simulates discrete raindrops advecting across the surface, {\ttfamily hydraulic\+\_\+stream\+\_\+log} is an Eulerian, stream-\/power erosion model. It operates globally across the grid using continuous \$\+D\+\_\+\textbackslash{}infty\$ flow accumulation, logarithmic discharge scaling, local gradient modulation, and hole-\/filling deposition.



This method carves continuous, smooth dendritic river valleys and dendritic drainage basins rapidly across large terrains without Monte Carlo sampling noise.

\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md51}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Continuous Flow Accumulation (\$\+D\+\_\+\textbackslash{}infty\$)}{Continuous Flow Accumulation (\$D\_\textbackslash{}infty\$)}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md51}
Water routing is computed using Tarboton\textquotesingle{}s \$\+D\+\_\+\textbackslash{}infty\$ continuous flow direction model. Rather than confining flow to one of eight discrete directions (\$\+D\+\_\+8\$), \$\+D\+\_\+\textbackslash{}infty\$ routes flow along triangular facets as a continuous facet angle \$\textbackslash{}theta \textbackslash{}in \mbox{[}0, 2\textbackslash{}pi)\$.

The flow accumulation \$\+A(x, y)\$ represents the total upstream drainage area draining through cell \$(x, y)\$\+:

\$\$\+A\+\_\+i = 1 + \textbackslash{}sum\+\_\+\{j \textbackslash{}in \textbackslash{}text\{inflow\}(i)\} w\+\_\+\{j \textbackslash{}to i\} A\+\_\+j\$\$

where \$w\+\_\+\{j \textbackslash{}to i\} \textbackslash{}in \mbox{[}0, 1\mbox{]}\$ is the fraction of cell \$j\$\textquotesingle{}s discharge directed toward cell \$i\$. Flow routing over flat regions or local depressions is stabilized using the reference slope parameter {\ttfamily talus\+\_\+ref}.\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md52}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Logarithmic Compression}{Logarithmic Compression}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md52}
Because upstream area spans several orders of magnitude (from \$10\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}0\$ at ridges to \$10\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}6+\$ at primary river trunks), standard linear stream power causes catastrophic over-\/carving in main valleys while leaving feeder streams untouched.

{\ttfamily hydraulic\+\_\+stream\+\_\+log} applies base-\/10 logarithmic compression to the accumulated flow\+:

\$\$\+A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{log\}\} = \textbackslash{}log\+\_\+\{10\}(A)\$\$

The compressed discharge is remapped to \$\mbox{[}0, 1\mbox{]}\$\+:

\$\$\+A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{norm\}\} = \textbackslash{}frac\{A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{log\}\} -\/ \textbackslash{}min(A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{log\}\})\}\{\textbackslash{}max(A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{log\}\}) -\/ \textbackslash{}min(A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{log\}\})\}\$\$

If {\ttfamily saturation\+\_\+ratio} \$\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<} 1.\+0\$, upper river branches are saturated to broaden valleys\+:

\$\$\+A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{sat\}\} = \textbackslash{}mathrm\{saturate\}(A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{norm\}\}, 0, s\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{ratio\}\}, 0.\+1 \textbackslash{}cdot s\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{ratio\}\})\$\$\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md53}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Gradient Modulation and Stream Power}{Gradient Modulation and Stream Power}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md53}
Stream power incision in nature depends on both discharge \$\+Q\$ and bed slope \$\+S\$\+:

\$\$E \textbackslash{}propto Q\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}m S\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}n\$\$

High\+Map models slope dependence by evaluating the smoothed surface gradient norm \$$|$\textbackslash{}nabla z$|$\$\+:


\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Compute gradient norm \$$|$\textbackslash{}nabla z$|$\$.
\item Smooth using compact pulse filtering ({\ttfamily smooth\+\_\+cpulse}) with prefilter radius \$r\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{grad\}\}\$.
\item Raise to exponent \$p = \textbackslash{}text\{gradient\+\_\+power\}\$ and apply a quintic smoothstep\+:
\end{DoxyEnumerate}

\$\$g\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{norm\}\} = S\+\_\+5\textbackslash{}left($|$\textbackslash{}nabla z$|$\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}p\textbackslash{}right)\$\$


\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Modulate flow intensity with the gradient scaling ratio \$r\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{scale\}\} \textbackslash{}in \mbox{[}0, 1\mbox{]}\$\+:
\end{DoxyEnumerate}

\$\$\+A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{mod\}\} = A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{sat\}\} \textbackslash{}cdot \textbackslash{}left((1 -\/ r\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{scale\}\}) + r\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{scale\}\} \textbackslash{}cdot g\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{norm\}\}\textbackslash{}right)\$\$\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md54}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Ridge and Peak Preservation}{Ridge and Peak Preservation}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md54}
To ensure that high peaks and sharp mountain ridges are not eroded away by accidental local flow initialization, relative elevation (\$\+RE\$) over a square kernel is sampled\+:

\$\$\+RE = \textbackslash{}frac\{z -\/ \textbackslash{}min\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}\+Omega\}(z)\}\{\textbackslash{}max\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}\+Omega\}(z) -\/ \textbackslash{}min\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}\+Omega\}(z)\}\$\$

Valley bottoms have \$\+RE \textbackslash{}approx 0\$, while sharp mountain ridges have \$\+RE \textbackslash{}approx 1\$. Incision intensity is masked by the inverted cubic smoothstep of \$\+RE\$\+:

\$\$\+A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{eff\}\} = A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{mod\}\} \textbackslash{}cdot S\+\_\+3(1 -\/ RE)\$\$

This focuses erosion strictly into valley troughs while preserving mountain crests and summits.\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md55}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Surface Incision}{Surface Incision}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md55}
Elevation is carved according to erosion rate \$c\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{erosion\}\}\$ and optional moisture weighting \$\+M(x, y)\$\+:

\$\$z \textbackslash{}leftarrow z -\/ c\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{erosion\}\} \textbackslash{}cdot M \textbackslash{}cdot A\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{eff\}\}\$\$\hypertarget{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md56}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{3. Deposition Mechanics}{3. Deposition Mechanics}}\label{md_docs_2content_2dev_2hydraulic__stream__log_autotoc_md56}

\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item {\bfseries{Pyramid Ladder}}\+: Generates \$\+L\$ power-\/of-\/two resolutions \$\textbackslash{}text\{ladder\}\mbox{[}i\mbox{]}\$ for \$i = 0, \textbackslash{}ldots, L -\/ 1\$, from coarsest to full resolution.
\item {\bfseries{Coarse Scale Carving}}\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The terrain is downsampled to {\ttfamily ladder\mbox{[}i\mbox{]}}.
\item {\ttfamily hydraulic\+\_\+stream\+\_\+log} runs on the coarse grid to identify primary macro-\/valleys.
\item The incision delta \$\textbackslash{}\+Delta z\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{coarse\}\} = z\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{before\}\} -\/ z\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{after\}\}\$ is calculated.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item {\bfseries{Domain-\/\+Warped Upsampling}}\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item \$\textbackslash{}\+Delta z\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{coarse\}\}\$ is upsampled smoothly back to full resolution.
\item Procedural Perlin domain warping ({\ttfamily warp}) perturbs the upsampled delta, preventing geometric blockiness and introducing natural organic meanders into the valley paths.
\item The scaled incision \$\textbackslash{}text\{mix\} \textbackslash{}cdot \textbackslash{}\+Delta z\+\_\+\{\textbackslash{}text\{warped\}\}\$ is subtracted from the full-\/resolution terrain.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item {\bfseries{Fine Scale Detail}}\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The finest level runs with {\ttfamily deposition\+\_\+scale\+\_\+ratio} active, producing detailed riverbanks, braided streams, and sediment flats.
\end{DoxyItemize}
