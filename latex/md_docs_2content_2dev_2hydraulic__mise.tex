{\ttfamily hydraulic\+\_\+mise} (Multiscale Implicit Stream-\/power Erosion) simulates long-\/term landscape evolution by solving coupled equations of fluvial bedrock incision, hillslope diffusion/debris flow, and alluvial sediment deposition across a dynamically re-\/routed drainage graph.



Unlike explicit particle tracing or simple height filtering, MISE uses\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\bfseries{Implicit integration}}\+: Unconditionally stable large time steps without numerical blow-\/up.
\item {\bfseries{Topological drainage graphs}}\+: Built using monotone priority-\/queue depression filling (Radix heap / bucket flood).
\item {\bfseries{Coupled sediment conservation}}\+: Eroded material is carried downstream as bedload and suspended sediment until equilibrium deposition conditions are met.
\item {\bfseries{Pyramidal multiscale scheduling}}\+: Macro-\/valleys are carved at coarse base resolutions and refined down to fine rills with domain-\/warped upsampling.
\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item A boundary queue seeds the flood, tracking minimum spill elevation.
\item Pits are identified and tagged ({\ttfamily lake}), grouping flooded basins and their associated spillway outlets ({\ttfamily lake\+\_\+outlet}).
\item Flow directions assign each non-\/outlet cell \$i\$ to a downslope receiver cell \$r(i)\$\+:
