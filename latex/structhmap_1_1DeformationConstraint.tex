\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Per-\/vertex penalty term used by \doxylink{namespacehmap_abec90e80c5d42318a7aadf6dd1016fec}{sls\+\_\+deformation}. 

Each constraint contributes {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_ad4a798b89d549031c1c431409a25a465}{scale} \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_af0e2e25f46be9570214dcfa57bc83083}{weight(x, y)} \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} (z(x, y) -\/ \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_a8f3ae7134e1a09cedb0f8e67a5a0040b}{target(x, y)})\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2} to the fitness of a vertex, gated by its type\+: for {\ttfamily \doxylink{namespacehmap_aaa31d3f4691447f824bf7ca1e88897a2aa5529116b7eb6406ffb42de58ae3314d}{MATCH}} the penalty always applies, for {\ttfamily \doxylink{namespacehmap_aaa31d3f4691447f824bf7ca1e88897a2a4a4ff914bd69d6b43e40b58f08627a3e}{ABOVE}} only where {\ttfamily z \texorpdfstring{$<$}{<} \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_a8f3ae7134e1a09cedb0f8e67a5a0040b}{target}}, for {\ttfamily \doxylink{namespacehmap_aaa31d3f4691447f824bf7ca1e88897a2a7e9c666cdfeb7b317902253fda25e978}{BELOW}} only where {\ttfamily z \texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_a8f3ae7134e1a09cedb0f8e67a5a0040b}{target}}. Vertices with a zero weight are unconstrained by that term. Several constraints are summed, so the {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1DeformationConstraint_ad4a798b89d549031c1c431409a25a465}{scale}} factor can be used to normalize competing terms.
