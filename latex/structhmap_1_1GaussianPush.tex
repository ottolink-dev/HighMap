\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Truncated-\/\+Gaussian push operation, the elementary deformation used by \doxylink{namespacehmap_abec90e80c5d42318a7aadf6dd1016fec}{sls\+\_\+deformation}. 

The push adds {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_a340fa2affea7c89d8575a66d832ab42b}{amplitude} \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} G(d)} to every vertex within {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_ab175ce3efeb574ba75f9d7e3f75c7b3a}{ir}} pixels of the center {\ttfamily (\doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_a01d24798bc375d4b997689c1a48447f4}{i}, \doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_a46e45b11a25e6e431318577e44f1a2e4}{j})}, where {\ttfamily G} is a truncated Gaussian kernel equal to 1 at the center and to 0 at distance {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_ab175ce3efeb574ba75f9d7e3f75c7b3a}{ir}}. The center vertex is therefore displaced by exactly {\ttfamily \doxylink{structhmap_1_1GaussianPush_a340fa2affea7c89d8575a66d832ab42b}{amplitude}}.
