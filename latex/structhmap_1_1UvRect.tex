\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Axis-\/aligned rectangle in normalized \mbox{[}0, 1\mbox{]}\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 domain/\+UV space. 

Represents the parametric sub-\/region of a tile or \doxylink{classhmap_1_1Array}{Array} within the unit terrain domain. (Halo cells may extend slightly outside \mbox{[}0, 1\mbox{]}.)
