Detailed Description

Truncated-Gaussian push operation, the elementary deformation used by sls_deformation.

The push adds amplitude * G(d) to every vertex within ir pixels of the center (i, j), where G is a truncated Gaussian kernel equal to 1 at the center and to 0 at distance ir. The center vertex is therefore displaced by exactly amplitude.
