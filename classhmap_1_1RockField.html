Applies a heavy-tailed power-law (Pareto) radius distribution to all rocks.

Radii are sampled according to the probability density $P(R \ge r) \propto r^{-\alpha}$, bounded within $[r_{\min}, r_{\max}]$.

Parameters

r_minMinimum rock radius.

)

Simulates gravitational slope sorting (scree/talus dynamics).

On natural scree and talus slopes, larger boulders carry more momentum and roll further downhill toward gentler base slopes, while smaller stones remain caught on steeper sections.

Parameters
slope2D slope gradient array (e.g., from gradient_magnitude or slope).
sorting_strengthStrength factor in [0, 1] governing the correlation between slope and rock size.
bboxDomain bounding box {xmin, xmax, ymin, ymax}.
