Cost metric model used for edge traversal.

Enumerator
EUCLIDEAN_2D 

Pure 2D Euclidean distance in the XY plane.

Edge cost is simply the 2D segment length: \( \text{cost} = d_{2D} \)

SYMMETRIC_Z 

Symmetric elevation change metric.

Treats uphill and downhill elevation changes equally: \( \text{cost} = |\Delta z| + w_{elev} \cdot z_{curr} \) where \( w_{elev} \) is elevation_weight.

HYDROLOGICAL 

Asymmetric hydrological flow metric for streams.

Strongly penalizes uphill steps while favoring natural downhill flow:

\[ \text{cost} = (1 - r_{elev}) \cdot d_{2D} + (\Delta z > 0 \;?\; p_{up} \cdot (\Delta z)^{e_{dist}} : |\Delta z|) + r_{elev} \cdot \max(0, \Delta z) \]

where \( r_{elev} \) is elevation_ratio, \(e_{dist} \) is distance_exponent, and \( p_{up} \) is upward_penalization.
